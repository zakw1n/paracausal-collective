## Needs a decision

1. Delete dead code: ContactForm, EnquiryForm, EventCarousel, EventTile, FooterContact, GalleryStrip, and the unused live `/api/contact` and `/api/enquiry` (anyone can use them to email the inbox).
2. Rate-limit `/api/subscribe` (Vercel firewall rule); consider BotID or a honeypot.
3. Cache-Control metadata on root R2 loops and photos.
4. Phone-sized video posters on R2 (main lever for gallery mobile LCP).
5. Showreel on phones: keep the 1080×1920 rung (~7.3MB/visit) or cap at 720p.
6. Pause control for autoplaying video (WCAG 2.2.2).
7. Optional: AVIF.
